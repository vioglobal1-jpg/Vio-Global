Sending a request does not create an order. A quotation is valid only for the period stated on it, and an order is confirmed only in writing by VIO.

Prices and shipping

Each quotation states what its price includes. Customs duties, taxes, clearance and local delivery are extra unless the quotation says otherwise.

Payment

Cancellation and deposit

The deposit is refundable until the order is confirmed with the supplier and moved to shipping. After that it cannot be refunded.

Delivery

Internal transport fees are added after delivery. Egypt and UAE stock can be delivered the same day within the same country.
